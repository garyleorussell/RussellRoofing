Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about residential roofing

Signs that you may need a roof replacement include: shingles that are curling, buckling, or missing; granules in gutters; daylight visible through the roof boards; sagging roof deck; and if your roof is 20-25 years old or older. Our free inspection can help determine if repairs or replacement is best for your situation.

Most residential roof replacements can be completed in 1-3 days, depending on the size and complexity of your roof, the materials used, and weather conditions. We'll provide you with a specific timeline during your consultation.

Yes, we offer manufacturer's warranties on materials and our own workmanship warranty. Depending on the materials you choose, manufacturer warranties typically range from 25 years to lifetime coverage. Our standard workmanship warranty covers installation for 10 years.

Homeowner's insurance typically covers roof damage caused by unpreventable events like storms or fires, but not damage due to normal wear and tear or lack of maintenance. We have experience working with insurance companies and can help guide you through the claims process if applicable.
