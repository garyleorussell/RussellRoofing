Commercial Roofing FAQs

Common questions about commercial roofing

The lifespan of a commercial roof depends on several factors including the type of roofing system, installation quality, climate, and maintenance. Generally, EPDM or TPO roofs can last 20-30 years, metal roofs 30-45+ years, and modified bitumen 15-20 years. Regular maintenance can significantly extend the life of any commercial roofing system.

Signs that your commercial roof may need replacement include: persistent leaks despite repairs, bubbling or blistering on the roof surface, significant membrane shrinkage, extensive ponding water, severe storm damage, or if the roof is past its expected lifespan. Our free inspection can provide a definitive assessment of your roof's condition.
